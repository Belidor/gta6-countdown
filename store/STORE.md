## Watch face

**Title:** GTA VI Countdown

```
Count down to GTA VI right on your wrist.

The watch face shows the time, the GTA VI logo and a live countdown to 19 November 2026: days, hours, minutes and seconds.

- Always-on mode on AMOLED watches: only the time and the countdown stay on, dimmed, and they move a little every minute against burn-in.
- Seconds tick while your wrist is up. In low-power mode the countdown updates every minute.
- On launch day it switches to OUT NOW and counts days since release.
- No permissions, no phone, no internet: everything runs on the watch clock.

Also available: GTA VI Countdown app with a glance for the glance list.

Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. GTA VI and its logo are trademarks of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc.

Source code: https://github.com/Belidor/gta6-countdown
```

## App

**Title:** GTA VI Countdown

```
Keep your favourite watch face and still see how long until GTA VI.

- Glance: one line in the glance list, for example "GTA VI 11d 04h 18m".
- Full screen: open it from the glance or with START to see the big logo and a live countdown with seconds. BACK closes it.
- On launch day it switches to OUT NOW and counts days since release.
- No permissions, no phone, no internet: everything runs on the watch clock.

The glance needs Connect IQ 4.0 or newer (for example Forerunner 965, fenix 7, Venu 2). On older watches the app is in the apps list.

Also available: GTA VI Countdown watch face.

Unofficial fan project. Not affiliated with or endorsed by Rockstar Games or Take-Two Interactive. GTA VI and its logo are trademarks of Take-Two Interactive Software, Inc.

Source code: https://github.com/Belidor/gta6-countdown
```
